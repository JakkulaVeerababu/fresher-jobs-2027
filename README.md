# Top 100 Companies for 2027 Fresher Software Engineers — India

> A free, community-maintained job search directory for B.Tech / BCA / MCA students graduating in 2027 looking for software engineering internships and full-time roles in India.
>
> Star this repo to get notified when new company portals and strategies are added!

---

## Who Is This For?

This directory is designed for:
- Graduating students (Class of 2025, 2026, 2027) looking for their first tech job
- Students with 0 years of full-time experience (freshers)
- Students from any branch — CS, ECE, IT, EEE, Mechanical, etc.
- Students targeting both on-campus and off-campus opportunities

---

## Important Notes Before You Apply

> Job posting URLs expire in 7-14 days. This directory intentionally provides Direct University / Early Careers portal links which remain active year-round. Search for open roles within these portals.

> On-campus placement cells may block you if you have less than 60% in 10th/12th. These portals let you apply directly to companies without going through your college placement cell.

## Strategic Roadmap for Fresher Applications

### Step 1: Apply to These 3 Companies First
- Zoho — Famous for ignoring school marks. Purely skill-based off-campus test.
- Atlassian — Sends automated HackerRank to almost all applicants. Pass the DSA round = interview.
- Morgan Stanley — Graduate Analyst program with structured hiring. Strong for high-CGPA students.

### Step 2: Apply on These Platforms (Not Just Company Portals)

| Platform | Why Use It |
|:---|:---|
| [Instahyre](https://www.instahyre.com/) | AI-matched jobs. Set skills and get recruiter inbound |
| [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/) | Startup jobs with direct recruiter messaging |
| [Unstop](https://unstop.com/) | 2027 batch filters, hackathons, off-campus drives |
| [YC Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) | YC-backed startups, no marks filter |
| [CutShort](https://cutshort.io/) | Curated Indian tech startup jobs |

### Step 3: Off-Campus = Bypass Your College Placement Cell
If your placement cell blocks you due to marks, apply directly using the career portal links in this directory. Off-campus applications go to global HR teams who do not apply the same 60% rule as campus coordinators.

### Step 4: Cold Outreach Template for LinkedIn
Find Engineering Managers at your target companies and send this:

> "Hi [Name], I recently applied for [Role] at [Company] (Job ID: XXX). I have built [mention one specific relevant project] and have [X]+ DSA problems solved. If you have a moment, I would love for you to glance at my GitHub: [link]. Happy to share more if this is relevant to your team's needs."
